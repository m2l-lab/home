Raneen Younis is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Peter L. Reichertz Institute for Medical Informatics, Hannover Medical School, Hannover, Germany. She received the B.Sc. degree in Computer Engineering from An-Najah National University, Nablus, Palestine, in 2015, and the M.Sc. and Ph.D. (Dr.-Ing.) degrees in Computer Engineering from Leibniz University Hannover, Germany, in 2019 and 2025, respectively. Her research interests include machine learning, data mining, time series analysis, graph representation learning, deep neural networks, explainable artificial intelligence, and multimodal learning, with applications to sensor data, medical imaging, and biomedical text.
