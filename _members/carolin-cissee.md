Carolin Cissée is a PhD researcher in the Medical Machine Learning (M2L) Lab at Peter L. Reichertz Institute for Medical Informatics. 
She holds an M.Sc. in Medical Information Management from Hochschule Hannover – University of Applied Sciences and Arts (2024). 
Her background combines machine learning and statistics with hands-on experience across a wide range of medical data types, including electronic health records, metabolomics, cancer classification systems, motion sensors, and ambulatory blood pressure monitoring. 
At M2L Lab, her research focuses on efficient representation learning for multimodal data, with a particular emphasis on time series.
