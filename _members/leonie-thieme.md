Leonie Thieme (née Basso) is a PhD researcher in the Medical Machine Learning (M2L) Lab at Peter L. Reichertz Institute for Medical Informatics. 
Her research focuses on analyzing volumetric medical images using AI, particularly in radiology, and uncertainty quantification of AI models to improve their reliability for clinical use.
She is part of the Lower Saxony Research Center for Artificial Intelligence and Causal Methods in Medicine (CAIMed), an interdisciplinary project on AI for personalized healthcare. Previously, she worked at the L3S Research Center on the topic of efficient AI methods for EHR and biosignal data, particularly in the intensive care unit use case. 
She holds an M.Sc. in Medical Informatics from Universität zu Lübeck with a focus on image and signal processing.
