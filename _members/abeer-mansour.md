Abeer Mostafa is a PhD researcher in the Medical Machine Learning (M2L) Lab at Peter L. Reichertz Institute for Medical Informatics, where she focuses on multimodal machine learning, graph neural networks, and computational histopathology. 
Her work centers on developing efficient methods for multimodal graph learning to tackle real-world challenges in healthcare applications.
She holds an M.Sc. from Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST), where her research focused on neural networks for human biometrics using inertial sensor data, and where she also gained hands-on experience as a Research and Teaching Assistant.

Before pursuing her PhD, Abeer worked as a Data Scientist at AItoAir, where she developed AI solutions with TinyML, exploring efficient machine learning methods for deployment on edge devices. 
She has also collaborated as an external researcher on projects spanning natural language processing and large language models in healthcare. Her goal is to advance healthcare by translating cutting-edge AI research into practical medical solutions that enhance patient care and improve health outcomes.
