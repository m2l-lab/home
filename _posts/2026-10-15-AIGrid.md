At the AI Grid Summit 2026, I had the pleasure to talk about the topic of our newest preprint, "Hidden Data Challenges in Clinical Machine Learning", in a deep dive session together with co-author Dennis Fast from TU Berlin. 
Using MIMIC-IV, we investigate how choices around timestamps, clinical endpoints, and missing data can affect data leakage, misalignment and what models actually learn.
